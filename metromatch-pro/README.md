# DateMarket - Know Your Worth, Find Your City

> The only dating market analysis platform that combines 917 US metros × 80+ demographic factors to show you where YOU rank highest.

---

## 🎯 Product Vision

**DateMarket** is a premium SaaS platform that uses proprietary demographic data to answer: **"Where should I move to maximize my dating market value?"**

Unlike generic dating apps or cost-of-living calculators, DateMarket provides **personalized market analysis** based on YOUR specific attributes (income, education, fitness, politics, height, preferences) to show where you'll have the most dating opportunities and highest competitive advantage.

